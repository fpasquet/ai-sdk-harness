First release: a person in the loop of AI SDK harness agents. `defineApprovalPolicy()` reads an approval policy, the same for every harness: shell commands allowed, asked about or denied by prefix — each command of a chain and of a substitution held to the rules, a redirection to a file never allowed —, file edits by path glob, tools by name, and a default for the rest. `agentSettings(harness)` gives a `HarnessAgent` the `permissionMode` and `toolApproval` that make it ask what the policy decides, `warnings(harness)` what a harness that cannot ask (Codex) does not follow, `evaluate()` what it makes of one call, `answer()` the responses a route continues a turn with, and `approver()` the `approve` option of `ai-sdk-harness-sessions`, which answers within the turn. "Always allow" for the rest of a session comes from `suggestGrant()`, kept with `withGrants()`: a grant allows what the policy would ask about, never what it denies. `describeToolCall()` tells what a call does for a person to approve it, and `answersOf()`, `isComplete()` and `describeAnswers()` answer the agent's questions, `askUserQuestions`. It runs in the browser too, for the `ToolApproval` and `QuestionsForm` components of the registry of the documentation.
